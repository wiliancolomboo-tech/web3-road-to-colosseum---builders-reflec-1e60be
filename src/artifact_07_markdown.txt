# Colosseum Retrospective: Zero-Downtime State Compression

### Key Architectural Learnings
During the sprint, our team tackled account space rent costs by migrating to state compression. 
We faced concurrency locks when writing multiple trees within the same transaction slot. 
By rearchitecting our instruction batching to isolate concurrent Merkle roots, we achieved 2,400 TPS 
on devnet with sub-second finality.
